# Proposal 001 — Naruto Uzumaki (v1, UNAPPROVED)

`proposal_v1.png` — generated 2026-11-07 from the two references described in
`docs/02_REFERENCE_NARUTO.md`. Shown to the user, **not approved, not locked, not
usable as a sprite**.

## What it gets right (checked against the references)

* Blond spiky hair, blue eyes, three whisker marks per cheek.
* Black forehead protector: long band, tails hanging at the back, blank metal plate.
* Black high collar, black shoulders and sleeves, black zip strip, orange body.
* Orange trousers, black ankle sandals, grey shuriken holster at the knee, black belt.
* Red Uzumaki spiral large and centred on the back.
* Front, back and action view plus a seven-swatch palette row.

## Where it is off-model (my own review, before you spend time on it)

1. **Jacket is inverted.** Canon Shippuden is *mostly orange* with black collar,
   black shoulder/upper-chest panel and black sleeves. Here the jacket reads as
   *mostly black* with an orange chest inset.
2. **The zip strip is pale**, not the black strip of the reference.
3. **White tee** appears only as a band at the hem; the throat/neckline should
   show it too.
4. **Sandals** are open-toe with individually drawn toes; canon shinobi sandals
   are closed black boots with a simple open front — this looks wrong at game
   size.
5. **Face is too adult**: square jaw, heavy brow. Naruto is rounder and younger,
   and the whisker marks are too faint in the front view.
6. **Proportions are ~8 heads / adult height**; the target is a lean ~166 cm
   teenager, closer to 7 heads.
7. **Knee holster** sits on the left leg in the front view and floats off the leg;
   the reference puts it on the right knee, strapped down.
8. **Fighting stance is not a fighting-game neutral**: very wide base, straight
   rear leg, weight behind. A versus-mode stance needs a compact base, both feet
   under the shoulders, front foot slightly forward.
9. **Hair tails** weave through the hair in the back view instead of hanging
   behind the neck.
10. Only one action pose. The dungeon camera (top-down, 4 directions) is not
    represented at all — it cannot be, until the versus design is approved.

## What v2 will change, unless you say otherwise

Items 1–9 above, keeping every "gets right" detail untouched. Item 10 waits for
approval of the versus design, as the pipeline requires.
